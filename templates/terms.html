What DocuSort is

DocuSort is a free, open-source tool that suggests a type for each document, extracts some details and marks personal numbers. It is a helper, not a records system, and it can be wrong.

Your responsibility

  • Check the type, the extracted details and any redaction before you rely on them or share a file. A redacted copy may still contain personal information the finder missed.
  • Only upload documents you have the right to process.
  • Don't use DocuSort as the only basis for legal, medical, financial or hiring decisions.
